About Bay Harmony

Bay Harmony Youth Foundation is dedicated to bringing people together and creating positive change in our community.

Who We Are

Bay Harmony Youth Foundation is built around the idea that positive change begins with people working together. We aim to create opportunities for community members to connect, contribute, and make a difference.

Our Mission

We work to make a meaningful difference through community, collaboration, and service. By bringing people together, we strive to support a stronger and more connected community.

Our Values

Community

We believe strong communities grow when people support and look out for one another.

Collaboration

We believe working together allows us to create greater opportunities for positive change.

Service

We believe meaningful service can make a lasting difference in the lives of others.
