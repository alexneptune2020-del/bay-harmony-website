About Bay Harmony

Bay Harmony Youth Foundation is dedicated to bringing people together and creating positive change in our community.

Our Story

Before becoming an organized nonprofit, [Music Studio Name] was established in 2015 in the San Francisco Bay Area with a passion for music education and community connection.

Over the years, the studio has grown to include five choir groups and nearly 100 piano and vocal students, ranging from children as young as three to adults in their forties. Through teaching and performing a wide variety of musical styles and genres, the studio strives to help each student discover their passion, develop their talents, and find joy in music.

Beyond music education, the studio has sought to bring people together through the universal language of music. By performing for seniors and sharing music with the broader community, we hope to spread joy, foster meaningful connections, and make music accessible to people of all ages.

Today, Bay Harmony Youth Foundation builds upon this foundation, carrying forward the belief that music can unite generations, strengthen communities, and bring people closer together.

Our Mission

Empowering Youth, Building Brighter Futures

At Bay Harmony Youth Foundation, our mission is to empower young people, foster personal growth, and create opportunities for a brighter future. We believe that every individual has the potential to make a positive difference, and that strong, connected communities begin with people supporting one another.

Through our commitment to growth, opportunity, and community, we strive to inspire individuals to discover their potential, build meaningful connections, and contribute to a more inclusive and harmonious society.

We envision a future where young people are encouraged to grow, individuals are empowered to make a difference, and communities thrive through understanding, collaboration, and shared purpose.

Our Values

Supporting Our Community

We believe a stronger community begins with people caring for and supporting one another. We strive to contribute to meaningful causes that make a positive difference in people's lives.

Standing With Those in Need

We hope to support individuals and families facing difficult circumstances, including those affected by cancer, through charitable giving and community support.

Building a Brighter Future

We believe that even small acts of generosity can help create a more caring, connected, and hopeful future for the communities we serve.
