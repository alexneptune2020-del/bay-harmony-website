Our Programs

Through music and community engagement, we aim to bring people together and share the joy of music with audiences of all ages.

Senior Center Performances

We visit a local senior center approximately every two to three months to share the joy of music through live performances.

Our performances feature group singing accompanied by piano, as well as solo piano performances. Through these musical experiences, we hope to create enjoyable moments, bring smiles to our audience, and foster meaningful connections through music.
